The problem

A consulting practice selling to a technical buyer who distrusts marketing language. The site had to read as serious to an engineering leader without becoming a wall of grey text.

What I did

  • Positioning built around one specific claim rather than a list of capabilities.
  • A didone headline on bone paper, with a mono status strip borrowed from instrument panels.
  • Copy written for a reader who will stop at the first unsupported superlative.

Where it landed

A different buyer from the rest of this page, and the same approach to earning attention.
