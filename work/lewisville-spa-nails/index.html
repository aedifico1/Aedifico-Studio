Lewisville Spa Nails

273 Google reviews and no website. The hero reads the hours table and tells you whether the shop is open right now. Below it sit a lookbook, real reviews, and directions.

The problem

A neighbourhood salon with 273 Google reviews and a 4.4 rating, and no website at all. Every one of those reviews pointed at a listing rather than a place the salon controlled.

What I did

  • The hero reads the hours table in JavaScript and states whether the shop is open right now, or when it opens next.
  • A lookbook of illustrated nail-art tiles stands in until the salon supplies photography.
  • Reviews are quoted verbatim with attribution rather than paraphrased into marketing copy.
  • Hours live in three places that must change together, and the README names all three.

Where it landed

A site that answers "are they open" before a customer picks up the phone.
